# Ray-camera denoising input contract

The first target is NRD's diffuse/specular radiance denoiser on the Vulkan ray camera. NRD requires primary-hit guides and a fresh noisy signal each frame; the camera result and radiance history are accumulated images. The standard Windows preset includes NRD. When denoising is enabled, Path Tracing and ReSTIR prepare view-Z, motion, and fresh diffuse/specular/excluded RGB images on each frame, dispatch RELAX, and composite its output before post-processing.

| NRD input | Current source | Remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World normal and linear roughness | `Camera.RayNormal` stores signed world normal in RGB and primary linear roughness in alpha. Anisotropic axes are reduced by geometric mean. NRD-enabled Path Tracing, ReSTIR Temporal Only, and ReSTIR Enhanced trace an additional pixel-center primary ray for stable guides while beauty samples remain jittered; Candidate and Spatial Only use the sampled primary hit. The guide pass packs the normal and roughness into `Camera.NrdNormalRoughness` using NRD's R10G10B10A2 octahedral formula. | Material ID currently uses zero. Subpixel geometry coverage can still differ between the guide and the sampled signal. |
| View Z | Path Tracing and ReSTIR store the primary world position in the 48-byte `RestirPtPrimarySurface`; `Camera.RayHitDistance` stores primary ray distance, which is not view Z. `RestirPtNrdGuides.slang` writes signed view Z and a finite out-of-range miss value to `Camera.NrdViewZ` in NRD-enabled builds. | Validate deforming geometry and unusual primary hits. |
| Motion | `RestirPtNrdGuides.slang` reconstructs previous world position from `previous_instances` and writes normalized non-jittered 2.5D motion, including view-Z delta, to `Camera.NrdMotion` in NRD-enabled builds. | Validate invalid history and deforming geometry. Skinned deformation requires previous-pose reconstruction. The rounded `screen_motion` used for ReSTIR pairing is not an NRD guide. |
| Diffuse and specular radiance | Candidate resolve routes its selected path by primary BSDF lobe. Spatial and Enhanced combine route each central/neighbor term before summing; the final resolve adjusts the split after outlier replacement and radiance clamping. Primary emission and background go to `Camera.NrdExcludedSignal`. The candidate also records primary diffuse/specular material factors. Resolve divides the noisy signals by these factors, and the composite multiplies the denoised signals by them. | Validate complex materials and reused-path behavior beyond Sponza. |
| Diffuse and specular hit distance | Candidate tracing records the first secondary hit distance for the sampled primary BSDF lobe, or the finite miss distance. Resolve places it in the matching signal alpha channel; RELAX uses 3×3 hit-distance reconstruction. `Camera.RayHitDistance` remains the camera-to-primary distance. | Validate reconstruction under spatial and temporal reuse, where the fresh candidate's lobe may differ from reused contributions. |

Denoising requires a fresh per-frame signal even when camera accumulation is enabled. Moving-camera and moving-object captures retain NRD history while raw camera accumulation resets. Compare denoised and raw output at equal GPU time.

## Pass boundary

ReSTIR combine writes per-lobe RGB while each source reservoir is known. Resolve adjusts those signals after outlier replacement and radiance validation but before accumulation. The guide pass derives view Z and motion from primary surfaces and previous-instance transforms; candidate tracing supplies the primary material factors and first-secondary hit distances. RELAX consumes demodulated fresh signals, and the composite remodulates its output before tone mapping.

Path Tracing and ReSTIR share the guide, RELAX, and composite passes. Path Tracing routes each fresh sample by its primary BSDF lobe before camera accumulation. ReSTIR Candidate tags its selected path; Spatial Only and Enhanced classify each contribution during combine, while its source reservoir is still known. Temporal Only classifies its merged reservoir during history resolve. Primary emission, unlit surfaces, and background remain outside the denoised lobes. The composite limits unusually bright excluded primary emission after the first accumulated frame, uses raw accumulated radiance for guide misses, and gradually blends raw radiance back in with denoised weight `min(1, sqrt(32 / accumulatedSamples))`.

RELAX uses 3×3 hit-distance reconstruction, 2-pixel diffuse and 8-pixel specular prepass radii, and two A-trous iterations. Path Tracing, Temporal Only, and Enhanced trace a pixel-center primary guide for stable view Z, normal/roughness, and motion; the beauty path remains stochastic. NRD history remains valid through camera and rigid-object motion when the input history is valid.

On the matched 1920×1080 Sponza setup against a 2048-frame Path Tracing reference, denoised PT64 has 0.02730 RMSE versus raw PT79 at 0.05260; denoised Candidate64 has 0.02922 versus raw PT120 at 0.04228. Enhanced64 has 0.03437 versus raw PT389 at 0.02175, so Enhanced still misses the equal-GPU-time quality gate. These budgets include ray-camera and NRD passes. Bright material-edge residuals, long-history energy differences, and Enhanced's high ray cost remain open. Fresh unaccumulated frames are still noisy.

NRD's [input contract](https://github.com/NVIDIA-RTX/NRD/blob/master/README.md#inputs) defines the guide and signal requirements. Its [SDK license](https://github.com/NVIDIA-RTX/NRD/blob/master/LICENSE.txt) governs integration and distribution of the library.

## Windows build

The standard `vs2026-x64` preset enables NRD and expects a source checkout under ignored `out/deps/NRD`. EvoEngine builds NRD with NRI and SPIR-V shaders and installs their runtime libraries and license files with its applications and runtime templates. Other configurations can still disable the build dependency with `EVOENGINE_ENABLE_NRD=OFF`. In an NRD-enabled editor, the camera inspector's **NRD Denoising** checkbox switches Path Tracing and ReSTIR Candidate, Spatial Only, Temporal Only, and Enhanced between raw and RELAX-composited output. Switching resets camera accumulation and denoiser history. Off skips the NRD guide, RELAX, and composite passes. Fresh raw-mode cameras do not allocate guide or denoised-output images. Temporal Only also uses raw shader variants for candidate, combine, and resolve that skip NRD signal work; other raw-mode shaders still prepare split signals. Demo captures can set `--preview-nrd-denoising enabled|disabled` to compare both outputs from one executable. The tested local checkout is NRD 4.18.0 at `7033ffbc` with NRI v181.
